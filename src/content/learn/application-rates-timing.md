## General rules

- Biologicals are **living products** — more is not better; correct is better.
- Apply in the **evening** or on overcast days; UV degrades spores.
- Don't tank-mix with chemical fungicides or bactericides.

## Common crops

| Crop | Product | Rate | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomato/Chilli | RhizoBoost Pro | 2 kg/acre | At transplanting |
| Tomato/Chilli | TrichoShield Plus | 2 kg/acre | 15-day intervals as drench |
| Cotton | NitroFix Max | 500 ml/acre | Sowing + 30 days |
| Rice | BioGrow Granules | 5 kg/acre | Before last puddling |
| Vegetables | NeemGuard Elite | 2 ml/L | First sign of pests, repeat 15 days |
| All crops | VermiGold Compost | 2–3 t/acre | Before sowing |

## Water quality matters

Chlorinated water kills inoculants. If your source is chlorinated, let water stand for 24 hours before mixing, or apply through non-chlorinated drip.

## Storage

Below 25°C, out of direct sun. Use within the printed expiry — CFU counts drop over time.
