## What are bio-fertilizers?

Bio-fertilizers are living microorganisms that help plants access nutrients — they don't replace fertilizer entirely, they make every kilogram go further.

## The main types

- **Nitrogen fixers** (Azotobacter, Azospirillum): convert atmospheric nitrogen into plant-available form.
- **Phosphate solubilizers** (PSB): unlock phosphorus locked in soil.
- **Mycorrhizae**: fungal partners that extend root reach for water and nutrients.
- **Potash mobilizers** (KMB): release potassium from soil minerals.

## How to apply

1. **Seed treatment** — coat seeds in a slurry before sowing.
2. **Soil application** — mix granules or powder with FYM and broadcast.
3. **Drip/drench** — liquid concentrates through irrigation.

## What to expect

Bio-fertilizers are not instant. Expect visible improvement in root growth in 3–4 weeks and meaningful yield impact within one to two seasons as the microbial population establishes.

## Do's and don'ts

- ✅ Store in a cool, shaded place — living organisms.
- ✅ Apply in evening or on cloudy days (UV kills microbes).
- ❌ Don't mix with chemical fungicides in the same tank.
- ❌ Don't use expired product — check the CFU count and date.
