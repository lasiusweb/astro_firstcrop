## From dependency to resilience

Ramesh Kumar farms 3 acres of tomato in Tamil Nadu. For years his costs climbed — more urea, more pesticide, same or lower yields.

## The turning point

A soil test in 2025 showed near-zero organic carbon and depleted microbial activity. Instead of adding more chemicals, Ramesh rebuilt the soil:

1. **Season 1**: Vermicompost (2 t/acre) + RhizoBoost Pro at transplanting. Root mass visibly doubled.
2. **Season 1, mid-cycle**: TrichoShield Plus drenching replaced preventive fungicide sprays. Damping-off losses fell from ~25% to under 5%.
3. **Season 2**: NitroFix Max replaced 40% of the urea schedule.

## The numbers

| Metric | Before | After 2 seasons |
|---|---|---|
| Yield (t/acre) | 12 | 21 |
| Fertilizer cost | ₹34,000 | ₹21,000 |
| Fungicide sprays | 9 | 3 |

## His advice

"Don't switch everything at once. Start with the soil — everything else follows."
